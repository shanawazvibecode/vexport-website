Operational Excellence

Quality Built at Every Station, Not Just the Final Dock

International buyers cannot afford container rejections or quality claims. At Vexport, every lot is tested against destination-market phytosanitary and food safety laws before the Bill of Lading is drafted.

  • Optical Sortex Grading & Calibration

    Triple-channel color sorters remove discolored seeds, chalky grains, and undersized produce with 99.8% precision.

  • Pesticide Residue & Heavy Metal HPLC Analysis

    NABL accredited laboratory testing confirms compliance with strict EU, US FDA, and GCC MRL (Maximum Residue Limit) thresholds.

  • Cold Chain Dataloggers in Every Reefer

    Continuous USB / Cellular temperature recorders verify that perishables stay strictly within calibrated temperature ranges.

  • Pre-Shipment SGS / Bureau Veritas Verification

    Independent 3rd party surveyor certificates provided upon buyer request prior to final customs seal dispatch.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions From Importers

Everything you need to know about our commercial trade terms and logistics.

What trade incoterms do you accept?
We trade under standard international Incoterms including FOB (Free On Board at JNPT Nhava Sheva or Mundra), CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight to your destination discharge port), and CNF. Payment terms are generally handled via Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at sight) or advance TT for container shipments.
Where are your procurement packhouses and warehouses located?
Our central aggregation packhouses and cold storage facilities are strategically located in Nashik and Lasalgaon (Asia's largest onion and vegetable market hub in Maharashtra), alongside dedicated collection terminals in Sangli (Turmeric & Spices), Unjha (Cumin & Seed Spices), and Punjab (Basmati Rice Belt).
Can you supply private-label packaging and custom sizing?
Yes, absolutely. We provide customized export-grade packaging including customer-branded multi-wall paper bags, poly-woven sacks, mesh bags, and barcoded retail pouches (500g, 1kg, 5kg) printed in Arabic, English, French, or your required local language.
How do you guarantee quality and prevent transit spoilage?
Every shipment undergoes mechanical Sortex grading, NABL-accredited pesticide residue screening, and pre-cooling before container loading. Perishable containers are equipped with continuous cellular/USB dataloggers to maintain temperature integrity from packhouse gate to your port.
What certificates are included with each export container?
Each container is dispatched with a complete document docket: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (COO), Phytosanitary Certificate issued by Indian Plant Quarantine, FSSAI / Spices Board quality certificates, Bill of Lading (BL), and optional 3rd party surveyor reports from SGS or Bureau Veritas.
